Master’s overriding authority

The Master has overriding authority and responsibility to make decisions concerning the safety of the vessel, crew, cargo and protection of the marine environment, and may request Company assistance whenever necessary. No forecast, traffic-light indication, commercial instruction or operational schedule shall override the Master’s professional judgement. The Master may delay, suspend, relocate or terminate operations where conditions or risks warrant. Any safety concern shall be reported promptly to the Company/DPA without prejudice to the Master’s authority.

Data and limitations

Marine model: Open-Meteo marine API. Wind model: Open-Meteo forecast API. The wave grid may be kilometres from the anchorage and may not resolve local shelter.

Start Bay buoy observations (Hs/Hmax): live when available, not a site-specific forecast. Authoritative UKHO chart: not connected. Estimated Hmax: 1.6–2.0 × model Hs, unvalidated screening heuristic only; validated forecast Hmax is unavailable.

Map attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenSeaMap contributors. Verify all anchorage coordinates, depths, hazards, chart licences, and marine forecasts before use.
