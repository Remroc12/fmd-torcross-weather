Which days were unsuitable for work?

Select a month to see each day classified using the observed Start Bay buoy Hmax threshold of 0.8 m. UNSUITABLE means at least one valid half-hourly observation recorded Hmax above 0.8 m. WITHIN LIMIT (OBSERVED) means all 48 expected observations were valid and none exceeded 0.8 m; this is not proof that the day was safe to work. INCOMPLETE / NO DATA means an exceedance cannot be ruled out. Wind and anchorage conditions are not assessed.

Source and limitations

Source: attached Start Bay quality-controlled wave files (2026 January–September), including the combined 2026 dataset. Only observations with QC flag 0 are included in statistics and the graph; all other flags are excluded and shown in the log. Data are historical and static: the report does not automatically update. Dates outside the supplied records have no data. Measurements are at the Start Bay buoy, not the Torcross anchorage. This is a historical reference, not operational clearance. The day classifications consider only buoy Hmax, not wind, tide, vessel motions or local anchorage conditions. An observation at exactly 0.80 m is within the specified threshold; a value above 0.80 m is an exceedance.
